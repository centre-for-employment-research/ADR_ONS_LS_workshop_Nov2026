ADR England Research Community Catalyst:Analysing extended youth transitions using the ONS Longitudinal Study (ONS-LS) and panel data models

As part of the ADR England Research Community Catalyst: Youth Transitions project, the University of Westminster is running a face-to-face event on 5th November to support you in applying a range of methods to administrative datasets. This session will introduce the ONS Longitudinal Study (ONS-LS), with a focus on using the longitudinal aspect of the data to study economic and social transitions – both within and across generations. The workshop will provide an overview of the datasets, technical details on how to manage and manipulate the data, and case studies of how longitudinal methods can be applied to this data.

The session will be led by Dr Emma Gorman, alongside Dr Min Zhang and Professor Peter Urwin, and will focus on practical insights, drawing on the session leaders' ongoing ESRC-funded work on intergenerational social mobility using the ONS-LS. The session will include presentations and group discussions which will provide an opportunity for participants to gain advice and support for their own research questions. We will also consider how to navigate the access and clearance requirements that come with the dataset, as well as the challenges of interpreting findings for policy development.
